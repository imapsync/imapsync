            Imapsync tips for ProtonMail imap server
=======================================================================



======================================================================
Q: Syncing from ProtonMail

R: To avoid a big destination folder "All Mail" with most messages 
   duplicated, use the option --skipcrossduplicates

    imapsync ... --skipcrossduplicates

======================================================================



======================================================================
Q: Syncing to ProtonMail

R: Use:

    imapsync ... --prefix2 Folders/
