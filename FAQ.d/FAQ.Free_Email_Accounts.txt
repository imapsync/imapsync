Q. List free IMAP email accounts providers compatible with imapsync.


A1. Gmail.
    15 Giga bytes.
    25 Mega bytes maximum for a single message.

A2. Outlook.com.
    15 Giga bytes.

A2. Yahoo. 
    1000 Giga bytes.

A3. AOL. 
    1000 Giga bytes.
    https://imapsync.lamiral.info/FAQ.d/FAQ.AOL.txt
    Be careful with AOL accounts, no activity during a year will
    delete all your emails definitively.  Sign in to your AOL mailbox
    at least once every 12 months to keep it active.
    https://help.aol.com/articles/empty-mailbox-in-aol-mail

A5. Mail.com 
    65 Giga bytes.
    Mail.com free accounts are not compatible with imapsync
    because POP3/IMAP usage is available exclusively to mail.com 
    Premium customers.

https://clean.email/best-free-email-service-providers
